Smoke is a port of the CS2 smoke volume pixel shader (`smoke-volume-shaders.ts`, constants in `smoke-field.ts`); label occlusion marches the same samples on the CPU, so change both together. The game uploads its density at a scale the shaders do not reveal: `smokeShaderDensity` (0.65 × √journal) is the one calibrated mapping, set so a quarter-percentile cell of a grown cloud transmits under 2% over 2 m while cores stay grey. The light volume is multiplied by π because the game's holds irradiance. Bullet tunnels use a 1.5 s lifetime, also an approximation. Each cloud bakes a 16³ light volume from the map's probe uniforms (`world/actor-lighting.ts`). [Smoke and weapon evidence](VERIFICATION.md#weapons-grenades-and-effects-2026-11-26)

First-person inspect, pin pull, knife heavy and plant clips come only from recorded evidence (`pov-actions.ts`): the inspect key bit in the recorded buttons, attack presses on a held grenade, secondary-only knife attacks and the C4 arming sample.
